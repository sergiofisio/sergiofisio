## About me

I'm a **Full Stack Developer with 5+ years of software development experience**, currently working at **Mobilemed Soluções Médicas**. My focus is on building maintainable applications, solving complex production issues, and improving performance and reliability across the stack.

- **Healthcare systems:** At Mobilemed, I work on critical teleradiology applications, troubleshooting production issues, optimizing databases, and evolving systems built with Angular, Node.js, and NestJS.
- **Business impact:** At Biointegral Saúde, I developed e-commerce and operational software. Workflow automation contributed to a **15% reduction in operating costs** and **2× team productivity**, as reported in my portfolio.
- **Product perspective:** My background in physiotherapy helps me understand real users, especially in healthcare, and turn operational problems into practical software solutions.

I enjoy working across frontend, backend, databases, and system architecture—and collaborating with international teams.
